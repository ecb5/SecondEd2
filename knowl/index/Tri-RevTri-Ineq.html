Lemma 6.2.2.

Triangle Inequality:
🔗
Let \(a\) and \(b\) be real numbers. Then
\begin{equation*} \abs{a+b}\leq \abs{a}+\abs{b}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Reverse Triangle Inequality:
🔗
Let \(a\) and \(b\) be real numbers. Then
\begin{equation*} \abs{a}-\abs{b}\leq\abs{a-b} \end{equation*}
