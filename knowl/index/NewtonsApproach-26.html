To compare 18th century and modern techniques we will consider Johann Bernoulli’s (1667–1748) solution of the Brachistochrone Problem. In 1696, Bernoulli posed and solved, the Brachistochrone problem: To find the shape of a frictionless wire joining points \(A\) and \(B\) so that the time it takes for a bead to slide down under the force of gravity is as small as possible.
