Problem 10.4.7. Upper and Lower Darboux Integrals.

Show that
\begin{equation*} \underline{\int^b_{x=a}}{f(x)}\dx{x}\le \overline{\int^{b}_{x=a}}{f(x)\dx{x}} \end{equation*}
🔗
Hint.
Let \(P^\prime\) be a fixed partition of \([a,b]\text{.}\) By Problem 10.4.6, \(U(P^\prime)\) is an upper bound for the set of all lower sums. This says
\begin{equation*} \underline{\int^b_{ x=a }}{f(x)}\dx{x}\le U(P^\prime)\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
However, \(P^\prime\) is an arbitrary partition of \([a,b]\text{.}\)
