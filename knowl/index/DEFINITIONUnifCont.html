Definition 10.4.12. Uniform Continuity.
Suppose \(S\subset \RR\text{.}\) We say that \(f(x)\) is uniformly continuous on \(S\) provided that for all \(\eps >0\text{,}\) there is a \(\delta >0\) such that \(\left|f\left(x\right)-f\left(y\right)\right|\lt \eps \) for all \(x, y\in S\) with \(\left|x-y\right|\lt \delta \text{.}\)
