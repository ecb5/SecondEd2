Definition 12.3.21. Simple Function.
A simple function \(s(x)\) defined on a measurable set \(E\) has two properties.
  1. The range of \(s\) is finite. In symbols, \(s\left(E\right)=\left\{a_1, a_2,\dots, a_n\right\}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The inverse image of each \(a_j\) is measurable. In symbols, \(s^{-1}\left(\left\{a_j\right\}\right)\) is measurable.
