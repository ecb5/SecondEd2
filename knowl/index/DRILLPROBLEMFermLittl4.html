Problem 1.3.7.

Use Problem 1.3.6 to show that \(p|(n^p-n)\) for all positive integers \(n\text{,}\) thus proving Fermat’s Little Theorem.
🔗
Hint.
Use induction on \(n\text{.}\) To get from \(n\) to \(n+1\text{,}\) use the binomial theorem on \((n+1)^p\) and the result of Problem 1.3.6 .
